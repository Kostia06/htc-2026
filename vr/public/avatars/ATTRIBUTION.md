# Interviewer avatar

`interviewer.glb` is `Business_Male_01` from the **Microsoft Rocketbox** avatar
library, converted from FBX to glTF with Blender (textures transcoded to JPEG,
morph targets pruned to the 27 the lip sync uses and renamed to Oculus viseme
and ARKit conventions).
