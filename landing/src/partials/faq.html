FAQ

Questions and answers

Do I still need to apply to jobs?

Yes. {{ brand }} doesn't replace applying; it adds what makes applications work: the right skills, a tailored resume, practice, and the people and events that get your name in front of whoever decides.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

A chatbot gives advice when you ask. {{ brand }} builds your Road from real Calgary job data, remembers what you've already done, and scores your interview answers so you can see yourself improve.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. Practice works on your phone and on the web. A Meta Quest 3 adds a virtual interviewer across the table, if you have one.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources: Job Bank open data and outlooks, Statistics Canada, the Alberta occupational outlook, and public job postings and event listings.

What happens to my practice and notes?

What you practise and finish is saved for you only. Crossroads shows anonymous outcomes from people heading to the same goal, never your name.

Which cities does it work in?

Calgary first. {{ brand }} was built at a Calgary hackathon and is tuned to local employers and events. Other cities come next.

How much does it cost?

Free while we're in beta.
